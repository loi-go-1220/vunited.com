Working across time zones is easy to get wrong. These five rituals have survived every reorg.

## 1. A written Monday kickoff

Everyone posts three priorities for the week. It takes five minutes and saves hours of status meetings.

## 2. Demo Fridays

Anything shipped — however small — gets a two-minute demo. Momentum is contagious.

## 3. No-meeting Wednesdays

Deep work needs protected time. We guard it fiercely.

## 4. Random coffee pairs

A bot pairs two people every fortnight for a 20-minute chat. No agenda.

## 5. Quarterly in-person days

Once a quarter, whoever can, meets up. Everything else stays async.
